# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Two roles: **students** (undergraduates, daily use, checking GPA/credits/alerts on desktop) and **teachers** (class advisors, weekly use, monitoring cohort health and managing interventions). Both are campus-native; they use WeChat, Feishu, and modern mobile apps daily—they recognise dated UI and will distrust a system that looks like a 2015 government portal.

## Product Purpose

ZJNU's AI-powered academic navigation system. Aggregates GPA trends, credit progress, five-dimension academic portraits, health reports, and alert signals into a single interface. Surfaces warnings early so advisors can intervene before students fall into academic crisis. Success means a student can open the dashboard, understand their academic standing in 10 seconds, and know exactly what action to take next.

## Brand Personality

Institutional confidence · campus warmth · quiet intelligence

Anti-adjectives: bureaucratic, flashy, clinical.

## Anti-references

- Generic Chinese government/university admin templates (dark-navy sidebar, red accent, dense tables, no breathing room).
- SaaS-cream dashboards (warm off-white bg, Inter, purple gradient buttons, metric cards with gradient text).
- Overloaded data dashboards (every pixel filled, no visual hierarchy).

## Design Principles

1. **Data at a glance.** The most important number on any screen should be readable in under two seconds. Hierarchy over decoration.
2. **Earned familiarity.** Use standard interaction patterns so the interface disappears into the task. Surprise is reserved for delight moments, not navigation.
3. **Institutional without institutional-izing.** ZJNU blue should feel authoritative and trustworthy, not stiff. Warmth comes through spacing and texture, not pastel color.
4. **Status is a first-class citizen.** Every alert, trend, and state change should be immediately legible. Color has one job: communicate state.
5. **Progressive disclosure.** Show summaries first; details on demand. Don't front-load all data.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG AA. Body text ≥ 4.5:1 contrast. All interactive elements keyboard-navigable. Reduced-motion alternative for all transitions.
